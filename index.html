2026 · Mechatronics design · Team project

Tracked Search & Rescue Robot

I was responsible for the mobility subsystem — designing the chassis and getting a small tracked robot to drive, turn and handle uneven ground.

Fusion 360 · 3D printing · N20 motors · Encoders · PI control

What I worked on
  • Designed a compact chassis with motor mounts, enclosed wiring and mounting points for the robot's other components.
  • Used two 100:1 N20 geared motors, rubber tracks and custom carbon-fibre-reinforced printed sprockets.
  • Worked with encoder feedback and PI motor control to improve straight-line movement and turning.
  • Tested the robot on bumps, ramps and turns. It reached 0.187 m/s, with an 8 mm lateral error over 0.8 m in our best recorded straight-line trial.

2026 · Robotics coursework

Vision-Guided Pick & Place

Used computer vision to find coloured objects, calculate their positions and guide a robot arm through pick-and-place tasks.

Python · OpenCV · HSV filtering · Homography

What I worked on
  • Detected objects using HSV colour thresholding, opening/closing filters and contour detection.
  • Found object centres and used homography to convert pixel positions to robot coordinates.
  • Connected the detection results to robot movement commands and pick-and-place sequences.
  • Tested in CoppeliaSim and on physical hardware, working through calibration and suction issues.
