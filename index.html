01 / MECHATRONICSTracked rescue robot

2026 · University team project

Search & Rescue Robot

I worked on the mobility side of a small rescue robot: the chassis, tracks, motors and how it moves around the course.

Fusion 360 · N20 motors · Encoders · PI control

What I did
  • Designed the compact chassis, motor mounts, component layout and enclosed wiring.
  • Used two 100:1 N20 motors with rubber tracks and carbon-fibre-reinforced 3D-printed sprockets.
  • Tested turning, straight-line tracking, ramps and bumps, and worked on motor speed control using encoder feedback.
  • The measured top speed was 0.187 m/s. It fell short of the high-speed target but performed well in several manoeuvring tests.

03 / ROBOTICSRobot vision & pick-and-place

2026 · University project

Vision-Guided Robot Arm

Used Python and OpenCV to detect coloured objects, work out their positions and send movement commands to a robot arm.

Python · OpenCV · Homography · Inverse kinematics

What I did
  • Detected colours and shapes using HSV thresholding, image filtering and contours.
  • Calculated object centres and mapped image coordinates into robot workspace coordinates.
  • Used inverse kinematics to move the robot to target positions.
  • Tested in simulation and the lab, including investigating real-world suction and calibration issues.
